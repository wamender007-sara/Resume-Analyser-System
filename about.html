About ResumeReviewer

ResumeReviewer is a 100% free, privacy-first career advancement platform engineered to empower job seekers—especially freshers, university graduates, and early-career candidates across India—to optimize their resumes for modern Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). In today’s hyper-competitive hiring landscape, over 75% of job applications are automatically screened and filtered by automated parsing software before reaching a human recruiter. ResumeReviewer levels the playing field by providing instant, transparent, and actionable diagnostic feedback.

Our intelligent platform performs comprehensive automated evaluations against real-world ATS benchmarks. It delivers precise keyword match scoring against custom job descriptions, identifies critical formatting and structural deficiencies, detects missing technical proficiencies, and validates section hierarchies. By benchmarking your profile against target industry roles, ResumeReviewer highlights exactly what technical skills and certifications you need to acquire to stand out.

Privacy and candidate security remain our foundational principles. Unlike traditional online resume services that upload and store sensitive personal records and contact details on external cloud databases, ResumeReviewer executes all document parsing, text extraction, and analysis entirely inside your local web browser. Your confidential information never leaves your personal device, guaranteeing zero tracking and absolute data sovereignty.

Built with passion to support Indian engineering graduates and young professionals navigating campus placements and competitive job markets, ResumeReviewer equips you with the insights necessary to bypass automated screening filters and secure dream interview opportunities.
